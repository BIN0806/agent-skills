# Clarify requirements

- Read the available conversation, relevant code and existing decisions first. Look up discoverable facts yourself. Identify the outcome and the unresolved choices that affect it.
- Ask a small round of independent questions with a recommendation and its consequence. Save questions that depend on an unanswered choice for the next round. Reuse answers already supplied, and continue until the requested decisions are settled or the missing information is clearly identified.
- Use concrete scenarios to uncover ambiguous terms, exceptions, conflicting requirements and ownership. Retain the project's established vocabulary and capture confirmed decisions in its existing documentation when that is part of the task.
- For an asynchronous questionnaire, establish the recipient and what information they hold. Give them enough background, group questions by decision, prioritize the important ones, and leave room for partial answers and uncertainty. Draft the document for the user to send.

Finish with the agreed requirements, assumptions and open decisions. A request for an interview ends at the agreed result. Carry an existing implementation request forward once its material uncertainties are resolved.
